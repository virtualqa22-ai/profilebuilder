# CareerVerve Brand Kit

Logo
Type: Clean, minimalist wordmark with an optional clever emblem (e.g., stylized “P” for “Profile” or globe/bookmark icon).
Variants: Horizontal (full) and vertical (icon-only) versions for versatile use.
Usage: Use only on plain or brand-compliant solid colour backgrounds; keep exclusion zones clear.

Colour Palette
Primary: Modern Blue (#3267E3 or #2563EB) – symbolizing trust, knowledge, and technology.
Secondary: Rich Teal (#17B1A8) or Emerald (#2BB572) – for call-to-action buttons and highlights.
Neutral: Ivory (#FAFAFA), Light Gray (#F5F7FA), Dark Charcoal (#23282D) – for backgrounds and type contrast.
Accent/Alert: Golden Yellow (#F4D03F) or Coral (#FF7F50) – for success/warning/alerts.
Note: Limit to 2-3 core hues per screen for a clean look. Sufficient contrast must meet accessibility guidelines (WCAG AA).

Typography
Primary Font: Sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Lato, Montserrat, or Rubik).
Hierarchy:
Headings: Bold, clear, slightly larger.
Subheadings: Medium or semi-bold.
Body: Regular/light, highly readable.
Web-safe alternatives: Arial, Roboto (fallback).

Iconography & Design Elements
Custom Icon Set: For resume sections (Profile, Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications, Languages, etc.).
Action Icons: Country flags, ATS status symbols (score check, alerts, pass/fail).
Design Notes: Rounded corners, subtle drop shadows, and simple geometric shapes help modernize the UI.

Voice & Tone
Style: Friendly, direct, and empowering—guides users without jargon or stress.
Consistency: Use second-person voice (“Add your experience”, “Select target country”).
CTAs: Encouraging but clear (“Boost your score”, “Save and Download”, “Check ATS match”).

Imagery
Stock Images: Diverse, professional workplace photos and illustrations. Avoid clichés.
Screenshots: Screens and UI mock-ups of the application for help articles, customer success, and demos.

Brand Guide & Assets
Usage Rules: When and where to use each logo, guidance for spacing/margins, proper colour values (RGB, HEX, CMYK for print/web).
Template Library: Branded docs—email headers, banners, cover images, shareable social assets.
All pages must be cross-platform, cross-browser and responsive compatible.
